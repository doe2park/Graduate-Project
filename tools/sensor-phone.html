How it is computed: for each window the per-axis mean is removed from the linear acceleration (x, y, z), and the RMS of the remaining magnitude is sent in m/s². This is acceleration, not the ISO 10816 velocity in mm/s; phone browsers sample at roughly 60–100 Hz, so vibration above about 30 Hz is not captured.
