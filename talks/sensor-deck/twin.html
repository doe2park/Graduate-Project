GRIMES

Grimes digital twin: candidate sensor tests

A short guide to the five pages that follow. Nothing is installed; every sensor reading in them is simulated.

Use → or the › button below to go to the next page. Base images and live views: May 6, 2026 360° capture of Grimes Level 1.

1

The project

•  We build a digital twin of the Grimes Building from 360° photo walks and the building’s BIM model.

•  The two are aligned, so pipes, ducts and equipment from the model appear in place in the photos.

•  Each sensor is attached to a BIM element ID, so a reading points to one pipe, duct or unit.

•  Next step: choose one or two wireless sensors for a small first test in the building.

The five pages

•  1. Fiber temperature on a sprinkler main

•  2. Airflow in a supply branch vs design

•  3. Wetness sensor at a pipe coupling

•  4. Power on a VAV circuit

•  5. Vibration on a fan-fed duct run

How to use each page

•  Left: a live 3D view of a Level 1 ceiling with the BIM drawn in. Drag to look around.

•  Right: the numbered sensors. Click one to see its simulated reading and chart beside it.

•  The live view takes about 10–20 seconds to load. Until then a still image of the same view is shown.

•  If it does not load, the Static view button keeps the still image with the sensors drawn on it.
