Source files and methodological details

Snapshot generation uses UTC; observations use America/Los_Angeles. The legacy aggregate timestamp is the last responding device timestamp, not proof that all devices were simultaneous. History keeps up to 96 observations and can span weeks. Missing values are not zero.

Estimates, anomaly signals and research limits

Energy, cost and carbon below are constant-demand scenarios: latest kW × 24 hours, then × $0.15/kWh or × 0.21 kg CO₂/kWh. They are not measured daily consumption, billed tariffs, avoided emissions or savings. The source’s next-demand estimate and anomaly flags are heuristic collector outputs, not validated forecasts or diagnoses.

Grimes combines responding BMO meters 3, 76 and 77. These are building / feeder readings. Brick relationships and provisional design-weighted allocations do not establish measured equipment consumption. No direct per-VAV or pump telemetry is supplied here. A missing meter is not a zero; source aggregates do not establish complete feeder coverage.

Floor count and building age are not used to claim efficiency. Occupancy, floor area and use are not controlled. Daily kWh needs interval energy or verified cumulative-meter deltas with reset/gap handling. Load factor needs valid energy and demand for the same period; EUI also needs floor area. Occupancy schedules and a defensible baseline are needed for after-hours or savings claims. These are not inferred from sparse kW samples.
