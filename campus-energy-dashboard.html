Estimates, anomaly signals and research limits

Energy, cost and carbon below are constant-demand scenarios: latest kW × 24 hours, then × $0.15/kWh or × 0.21 kg CO₂/kWh. They are not measured daily consumption, billed tariffs, avoided emissions or savings. The source’s next-demand estimate and anomaly flags are heuristic collector outputs, not validated forecasts or diagnoses.

Grimes combines responding BMO meters 3, 76 and 77. These are building / feeder readings. Brick relationships and provisional design-weighted allocations do not establish measured equipment consumption. No direct per-VAV or pump telemetry is supplied here. A missing meter is not a zero; source aggregates do not establish complete feeder coverage.

Floor count and building age are not used to claim efficiency. Occupancy, floor area and use are not controlled. No savings or causal performance conclusions are inferred from these observations.

Data sources and collection method

BMO CSV exports → scheduled GitHub Actions → public JSON on the data branch. A 15-minute schedule is a collection target, not a guarantee of fresh readings. This page checks the published snapshot every minute. Snapshot generation uses the collector’s UTC clock; BMO observations use Pacific wall time. Date-only or ambiguous local times are marked unavailable rather than guessed.

The history retains up to 96 collector observations per building. It can span much longer than 24 hours. Windows end at the current time; chart gaps over 60 minutes are not interpolated. Sample mean is unweighted and is not a time-weighted energy estimate.
