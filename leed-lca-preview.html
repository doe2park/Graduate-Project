LEED + LCA Panel Redesign — Before / After

Two pieces of LEED submittal data (operational-energy from bechtel_leed.json, structural-reuse from bechtel_lca.json) get rendered into the Grimes popup in two modes: Engineer (right-docked SCADA panel) and Public (bottom-center storytelling card). The previous pass got the data wired up but the panels were text-heavy. This pass replaces the text with donut, stacked bars, comparison bars, building cross-section SVG, and icon-led element rows. Real numbers from Bechtel renovation throughout.
